Recovery and support

  • Automatic recovery keeps verified local checkpoints in origin-private storage.
  • Named versions are immutable and restore as a new document.
  • Diagnostics exclude names, text, pixels, paths, URLs and source bytes; nothing is uploaded.

Known limitations

  • Editing is 8-bit RGB; 16/32-bit input is converted for editing.
  • CMYK and Lab are converted to RGB and are not saved as native modes.
  • Safari has a limited support tier; use Chrome, Edge or Firefox for large work.
  • Cloud accounts, sharing and collaboration are not available in this local build.
  • Photoshop warning-free and 1,000-file corpus acceptance are still external release gates.
